Caring for Your Aari Work

Your handcrafted piece is an investment. With proper care, it will remain beautiful for years to come.

Care Instructions

Dry Clean Only

Always use professional dry cleaning. Hand washing risks damage to beads, threads, and lining.

Avoid Excessive Moisture

Keep your piece away from rain, spills, and high humidity. Store in a dry place.

Store Flat or Folded with Soft Cloth

Fold your blouse or piece with acid-free tissue paper or clean cotton cloth. Store in a breathable cloth bag to prevent dust and moisture.

Avoid Direct Ironing on Embroidery

Never iron directly on embroidered areas. If ironing is needed, use low heat and a cloth barrier between iron and fabric. Better yet, steam from a distance.

Our Support Terms

We Fix Manufacturing Defects

Minor finishing concerns must be reported within 7 days of delivery. We will repair defects like loose beads or unraveling stitches at no extra cost.

We Don't Cover Damage from Use

Damage due to wear, washing (other than professional dry cleaning), stretching, or mishandling is not covered. This is a handcrafted piece that requires proper care.

Report Issues Quickly

If you notice any quality concerns after delivery, contact us within 7 days with photos. This allows us to inspect and address issues promptly.

Long-Term Care Tips

For Blouses & Daily Wear

  • • Wear only for special occasions to reduce friction
  • • Remove jewelry that might catch on threads
  • • Avoid wearing during high-humidity weather
  • • Air out after wearing before storing

For Sarees & Dupattas

  • • Store with pallu folded outward (protected)
  • • Use naphthalene balls in sealed storage
  • • Refold periodically to prevent creases
  • • Inspect annually for any loose threads
