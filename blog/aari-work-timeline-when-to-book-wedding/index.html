How long does Aari work take and when should you book?

Custom embroidery has several steps beyond stitching. Use this timeline guide to plan your blouse order around design approval, fabric readiness and your wedding date.

“How long does Aari work take?” has no single answer. A small border and a densely embroidered bridal blouse are different projects, and turnaround also depends on the artisan's current schedule, material availability and how quickly design decisions are confirmed. Only the studio handling your order can give you a dependable completion estimate.

A practical planning window: start early

As a planning guideline not a guaranteed production time contact your embroiderer around 8–12 weeks before the wedding when possible. This gives room to discuss the design, confirm a quote, prepare the blouse or fabric, complete the work and arrange any fitting or adjustments. Ask for the studio's current lead time as soon as you know the event date; busy wedding seasons may book up earlier.

If your blouse is already stitched or your design is simple, the process may be shorter, but do not assume there is availability. For complex, full-coverage or multi-piece orders, allow more planning time and get a schedule confirmed before you commit.

What affects an Aari embroidery timeline?

  • Design scale and stitch density: Large motifs and dense coverage require more handwork than a small neckline or sleeve border.
  • Number of pieces: Multiple blouses or matching garments need coordinated planning.
  • Material and design approvals: Changes made after work starts can add steps; late decisions may delay the agreed schedule.
  • Fabric readiness: Confirm whether the studio needs unstitched fabric, a stitched blouse or a pattern/template, and when it must arrive.
  • Fittings and finishing: Leave time for tailoring, alterations, inspection and safe packing before travel or the event.
  • Seasonal workload: Wedding dates cluster, so a studio's available capacity varies throughout the year.

Work backwards from your wedding date

  1. As early as possible: Share your date, saree details, reference images and budget preferences; ask about availability and lead time.
  2. Before production: Finalise design placement, colours, materials, coverage, quote and the date the studio expects the blouse or fabric.
  3. During the work: Respond promptly if a design clarification or approval is needed.
  4. Before the event: Reserve time for fitting, final checks and collection or delivery. Avoid scheduling completion for the day before travel.

What if the wedding is soon?

Contact the studio with the exact event date and ask honestly whether the work can be completed. A simpler design, fewer embroidered areas or an already-prepared blouse may be more feasible, but the decision depends on current workload. Do not book based on a general estimate alone; get a realistic completion date and clarify what is included.
