Emerald Jhumka Elegance Blouse: Materials, Styling and Timing

Emerald Jhumka Elegance begins with a deep emerald base that seems to hold the light rather than reflect it. At the front, a rounded neckline is framed by a rhythm of gold oval motifs, repeated along the sleeve cuffs and lower border for a beautifully composed finish. Turn, and the wide U-shaped back reveals jhumka-style tassels that sway softly as you move.

The silhouette is classic and flattering, made to sit gracefully under a silk saree without competing with it. Emerald Jhumka Elegance FAQs

What occasion is Emerald Jhumka Elegance best for?

Engagements, receptions, sangeet and festive family functions. The jhumka tassels on the back and the gold oval motifs give a traditional look without full bridal weight.

Which sarees go well with Emerald Jhumka Elegance?

Bottle green, emerald, gold and warm cream silk match it most naturally. A bright red or deep maroon saree makes the gold ovals stand out. Send us a daylight photo of your saree and we will confirm the match.

Can I keep the tassels but change the back neckline?

Yes. The jhumka tassels can be added to most back necklines. Tell us the shape you want and we will confirm how the oval border will sit around it.

Can you change the colours or motifs on Emerald Jhumka Elegance?

Yes. Most brides change something: the thread colour to match their saree, a denser or lighter fill, or a different neckline and sleeve finish. Send your saree photo on WhatsApp and we will tell you what works and whether it changes the price.

How do I order Emerald Jhumka Elegance?

Tap WhatsApp or Make It Yours and send your saree photo and event date. We confirm the design, price, courier charge and delivery date. You pay a 50% advance, courier your blouse to our Trichy studio, and we send it back by tracked courier once the work is done.
