Violet Gold Vine Blouse: Materials, Styling and Timing

Violet Gold Vine is a study in quiet opulence. A rich violet base is traced with antique-gold Aari floral vines and leaf motifs that travel from the neckline to the sleeve edges and blouse borders, their curves echoed by fine linear stone embellishment across the sleeves. The back carries a coordinating detail, so the design reads beautifully from every side. It is a blouse for the woman who prefers her luxury understated, and it grows more beautiful the closer you look.

Violet flatters across skin tones, and it pairs wonderfully with purple, gold, cream or a considered contrast in silk. The botanicals keep the look romantic; the stonework keeps it occasion-ready.
