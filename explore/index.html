Aari Blouse Design Questions

What types of Aari blouse designs are available?

Khadeeja Designs showcases different styles of handcrafted blouse embroidery, including bridal-inspired designs, floral motifs, traditional patterns and decorative gold embroidery. Browse our collection to explore available designs.

What is the price range of Aari work blouses?

The price of an Aari work blouse depends on the design, embroidery coverage, materials and level of detailing. Each design in our portfolio displays its listed price. Customised requirements may have a different quotation depending on the requested work.

Are Aari blouse designs handmade?

Khadeeja Designs focuses on handcrafted Aari embroidery. Each order is planned according to the selected design and embroidery requirements. Contact us if you would like to know more about the techniques used for a particular design.

Can I customise the colours and embroidery patterns?

Yes, you can discuss changes to embroidery colours, motifs, placement, neckline details and sleeve styles. Share your reference image or preferred design so we can understand your requirements.

Which Aari blouse designs are suitable for silk sarees?

The right Aari blouse design depends on your saree colour, fabric, border, pallu and jewellery. Traditional gold embroidery, floral motifs and carefully selected decorative patterns can complement silk sarees. We can help you discuss suitable design options.

Can I order a design shown in the portfolio?

You can enquire about any design displayed in our portfolio. Select your preferred design and use the enquiry option to share your requirements. We will discuss availability, customisation and pricing before confirming your order.

Can bridal Aari blouse designs be customised?

Yes, bridal designs can be discussed for customisation according to your preferred colours, motifs, neckline, sleeve details and embroidery coverage. Share your saree details and wedding requirements so we can understand your vision.

How do I enquire about a particular Aari blouse design?

Open your preferred design, review its details and select the Make It Yours option. You will be directed to our enquiry page, where you can share your contact information and design requirements. We aim to respond within 48 hours.
