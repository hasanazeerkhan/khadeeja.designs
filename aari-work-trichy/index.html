Simple process

How ordering works

  1. Step 01

    Share your brief

    Send your saree photo, occasion, event date and any reference on WhatsApp or through the enquiry form.

  2. Step 02

    Get your quote

    We confirm the design, the embroidery price, the courier charge and a delivery date for your event.

  3. Step 03

    Pay the advance and post your blouse

    Pay a 50% advance and courier your blouse or saree to our Trichy studio.

  4. Step 04

    We embroider and ship it back

    Your piece is hand-embroidered in about 14–21 days. Pay the balance and we send it back by tracked courier.

Questions from Trichy customers

Where is your Aari studio in Trichy?

Our studio is at 17 East Sayakara Lane, Jaffersha Street, Tiruchirappalli (Trichy) 620008, Tamil Nadu. Open it in Google Maps for directions.

Can I visit the studio?

Yes, by appointment. Message us on WhatsApp with a day and time and we will confirm. Bring your blouse, your saree and any reference photos so we can match threads and motifs in person.

How much does Aari work cost in Trichy?

Our listed designs start at ₹1,999 for light work and go up to ₹8,499 for heavy bridal work, for the hand embroidery on a blouse you supply. See the price guide for what each level includes.

How long does it take?

About 14–21 days at the frame once we have your blouse and the 50% advance. For a wedding, book 4–6 weeks ahead.

I'm not in Trichy. Can I still order?

Yes. Customers across Tamil Nadu and India courier their blouse to us and get it back by tracked courier. Delivery within Trichy is free; courier elsewhere is quoted separately. See how ordering works.
