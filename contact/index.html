How ordering works

  1. Step 01

    Share your brief

    Send your saree photo, occasion, event date and any reference on WhatsApp or through the enquiry form.

  2. Step 02

    Get your quote

    We confirm the design, the embroidery price, the courier charge and a delivery date for your event.

  3. Step 03

    Pay the advance and post your blouse

    Pay a 50% advance and courier your blouse or saree to our Trichy studio.

  4. Step 04

    We embroider and ship it back

    Your piece is hand-embroidered in about 14–21 days. Pay the balance and we send it back by tracked courier.

Before You Send an Enquiry

How can I order Aari embroidery from Khadeeja Designs?

Send your saree photo and event date on WhatsApp, or fill in the form above. We confirm the design, price, courier charge and delivery date. You then pay a 50% advance and courier your blouse to our Trichy studio. Full terms are on the order and refund policy page.

Do you accept customised bridal blouse orders?

Yes, you can discuss customised bridal blouse embroidery according to your preferred colours, motifs, neckline and sleeve details. Share your reference image and event date to help us understand your requirements.

Can I share my design reference?

Yes, you can share design reference images and your requirements on WhatsApp (easiest for photos), Instagram, email or the enquiry form. This helps us understand your preferred embroidery style before discussing the quotation.

Do you accept Aari embroidery orders from other cities?

Yes, Khadeeja Designs is based in Trichy, Tamil Nadu, and offers delivery across India. Contact us to discuss your requirements and shipping arrangements.

How soon will I receive a response to my enquiry?

We reply within 6 hours during studio hours (10 am to 7 pm, Mon to Sat (Sunday by appointment)). Please share your design requirements, preferred timeline and contact information so we can assist you. See the full FAQ page for more answers.
