Before You Send an Enquiry

How can I order Aari embroidery from Khadeeja Designs?

Browse our design collection, select a design you like and use the enquiry option to share your requirements. You can also contact us through Instagram or email to discuss a customised order.

Do you accept customised bridal blouse orders?

Yes, you can discuss customised bridal blouse embroidery according to your preferred colours, motifs, neckline and sleeve details. Share your reference image and event date to help us understand your requirements.

Can I share my design reference?

Yes, you can share design reference images and your requirements through Instagram, email, or the enquiry form. This helps us understand your preferred embroidery style before discussing the quotation.

Do you accept Aari embroidery orders from other cities?

Yes, Khadeeja Designs is based in Trichy, Tamil Nadu, and offers delivery across India. Contact us to discuss your requirements and shipping arrangements.

How soon will I receive a response to my enquiry?

We aim to respond to enquiries within 48 hours. Please share your design requirements, preferred timeline and contact information so we can assist you. See the full FAQ page for more answers.
