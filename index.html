How to Start Your Order

1

Send Your Inquiry & Schedule Consultation

Contact us with your requirements: type of blouse, design inspiration, color preferences, and event timeline. You can also book a 15-minute design consultation (optional) to refine your ideas with our experts who will help you choose the perfect design, neckline, and embroidery placement based on your saree and body type.

📧 Email us, fill out our contact form, or schedule a consultation call. Consultation fee: ₹499 (redeemable against your order).

2

Receive Design Mockup, Quote & Approve

We'll send you a detailed design mockup and transparent pricing based on your specifications, including embroidery placement, stitch density, and material costs. Review everything and let us know if you'd like any changes or if you're ready to move forward.

🎨 High-quality visual mockup of your custom design. Transparent pricing with no hidden charges.

3

Secure Your Order with Booking Payment

Once you approve the design, we'll collect a 50% booking payment to secure your spot and lock in your delivery timeline. This is when your production schedule officially begins.

💳 Flexible payment options available. Timeline starts after booking confirmation.

4

Expert Handcrafted Production

Your blouse enters our production timeline with our expert artisans hand-stitching your custom design with precision and care. Timeline depends on complexity: 10-14 days for simple designs, 18-25 days for bridal work, and 25-35 days for heavy couture pieces. We provide progress updates throughout.

⏱️ Transparent timelines based on complexity. Regular updates on your order status.

5

Quality Check, Final Payment & Delivery

We perform a thorough quality check before shipment. You'll receive photos of the finished blouse for your approval. Pay the remaining 50% and your custom piece is carefully packaged and shipped to you with detailed care instructions and lifetime after-care support.

✅ Every stitch inspected. Secure packaging with tracking. Lifetime care consultation available.

Important Notes

Limited Capacity: We accept a limited number of orders per week to maintain our quality standards.

Event-Date Assurance: If you have a specific event date, let us know early. We may be able to prioritize your order for an additional fee.

Design Confirmations: Timelines begin only after you approve the design and make the booking payment.

Custom Work: Each blouse is handcrafted to order. No stock items—everything is made specifically for you.
