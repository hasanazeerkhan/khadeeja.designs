About & FAQ

Below are the common questions we kept getting — no vague marketing talk. Straight answers so you can decide fast.

What is Aari embroidery?

Aari is a hooked-needle technique producing chain stitch motifs. It's used for dense, textured embroidery with beads and sequins. We follow sourced materials and traditional methods adapted for durability.

Do you take custom bridal orders?

Yes. Bridal orders include a design consultation, sample swatch, stitching schedule, and mid-way quality check. Expect 2–3 rounds of revisions during the design stage.

How long does a typical project take?

Small bespoke pieces (1–2 panels): 7–10 days. Full bridal sets: 14–28 days depending on beadwork and bead density. Rush orders available at a surcharge — ask first.

What materials do you use?

We use high-twist silk threads, glass and crystal beads, and reinforced base fabrics (silk, velvet, organza). If you request alternative threads or eco-materials, we can source them on demand.

Can you match an existing design?

Yes. Provide clear photos and fabric swatches. We produce a small sample swatch first to confirm thread color, density and bead placement before the main run.

Pricing & payment

Pricing depends on stitch-density, bead count and fabric. Typical small custom pieces are priced per panel. We take 50% advance, remaining on completion and approval. International payments accepted via bank transfer or Stripe.

Quality assurance & returns

Every piece is quality-inspected for stitch integrity and finish. We repair manufacturing defects within 30 days. We do not accept returns for custom work changed after approval; read the order terms before signing off.

Do you offer bulk or production runs?

Yes — for designers and boutiques. Lead times and minimum quantities vary. Contact us to discuss sample-to-production timelines, pricing tiers and logistics.

How do shipping & packaging work?

We pack items with acid-free tissue and fabric-safe boxes. Domestic courier with tracking is included by default; international shipping is calculated separately and insured on request.
