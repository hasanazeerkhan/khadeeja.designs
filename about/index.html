About Us

A boutique Aari embroidery studio in Trichy creating custom bridal blouses, heirloom details, and carefully crafted embroidery for women who want each stitch to feel personal.

Studio Trichy
Craft Hand Aari
Focus Custom

Khadeeja Designs is rooted in the artistry of Aari embroidery, with each piece designed to balance tradition, craftsmanship, and modern bridal styling.

From bridal blouses to special-occasion garments, the studio focuses on custom work that begins with your reference, your silhouette, and the mood you want your final piece to carry. Every order is discussed closely so the embroidery feels considered, not generic.

We work on customer-supplied garments, keeping the process transparent from the first enquiry to the final finishing details. Designs are handcrafted in studio and tailored to the garment you already love.
