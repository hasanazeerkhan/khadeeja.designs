How we work

Order, advance & refund policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Every Khadeeja Designs piece is hand-embroidered to order on the blouse or saree you send us. This page sets out what you pay, when, and what happens if plans change. If anything here is unclear, ask us on WhatsApp before you pay.

What the price covers

  • Listed prices are for the hand Aari embroidery shown in the design, done on the blouse or garment you supply.
  • The blouse or fabric itself, blouse stitching and extra materials beyond the listed design are not included.
  • Delivery within Trichy is free. For other places, courier charges vary by location and are quoted separately.
  • Your written quote on WhatsApp or email lists the embroidery price, any changes you asked for, the courier charge and the delivery date. Nothing is charged before you accept it.

Payment

  • 50% advance once you accept the quote. Work is scheduled only after the advance is received.
  • Balance 50% when the embroidery is finished. We send you photos of the finished piece first, then dispatch it after the balance is paid.
  • We accept UPI and bank transfer. We may send a payment link for your quote; we will never ask for card details or an OTP on WhatsApp.

Timeline

  • Most pieces take about 14–21 days at the frame after we receive your blouse and the advance. Heavy bridal work can take longer; your quote states the date.
  • Courier time is extra: usually 2–4 days each way within India.
  • For weddings, enquire at least 4–6 weeks before the event.

Sending your blouse

  • We share the studio address with your order confirmation.
  • Fold the blouse inside a clean plastic cover, put it in a padded envelope or box, and include a note with your name, phone number and design name.
  • If you want the work placed to fit you, send a well-fitting blouse as a measurement reference or share your measurements on WhatsApp.
  • Use a tracked courier and send us the tracking number. We message you when it arrives.

Cancellation and refunds

  • Before work starts: cancel before we begin embroidering and we refund your advance minus any courier we have already paid, and return your blouse at your cost.
  • After work starts: the advance is not refundable, because the embroidery is made for your garment and cannot be resold. We return your blouse with the work completed so far.
  • Refunds are paid back to the original UPI or bank account within 7 working days.

Rework and quality

  • Check your piece when it arrives. If something does not match the agreed design (a missed motif, loose stitching, wrong thread colour against the approved sample), tell us on WhatsApp within 7 days of delivery with photos.
  • We fix agreed-design issues free of charge, including return courier.
  • Changes to the design after it was approved are treated as a new request and quoted separately.
  • Hand embroidery has small, natural variations from the photos on this site. That is part of handmade work and is not a defect.

Returns

Because each piece is embroidered on your own garment to your brief, we cannot accept returns for a change of mind. The rework and cancellation terms above apply instead.

Delivery back to you

Delivery within Trichy (Tiruchirappalli) is free. We ship anywhere else in India by tracked courier and send you the tracking link on dispatch. We are not responsible for courier delays, but we will help you follow up with the courier company.
