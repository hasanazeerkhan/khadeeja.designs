How to Start Your Order

A simple, transparent process to bring your custom Aari embroidery vision to life. From initial inquiry to final delivery.

The Ordering Process

1

Send Your Inquiry

Contact us with your requirements: the type of blouse, design inspiration, color preferences, and event timeline.

📧 Email us or fill out our contact form with details about your vision.

2

Schedule a Consultation (Optional)

Book a 15-minute design consultation to refine your ideas. Our experts will help you choose the perfect design, neckline, and embroidery placement based on your saree and body type.

💬 Video call or phone consultation available. Consultation fee: ₹499 (redeemable against your order).

3

Receive Design Mockup & Quote

We'll send you a detailed design mockup and pricing based on your specifications. This includes embroidery placement, stitch density, and material costs.

🎨 High-quality visual mockup of your custom design. Transparent pricing with no hidden charges.

4

Design Approval & Booking Payment

Once you approve the design, we'll collect a 50% booking payment to secure your spot and lock in your delivery timeline.

💳 Flexible payment options available. Timeline begins after booking confirmation.

5

Handcrafted Production

Your blouse enters our production timeline. Our expert artisans begin hand-stitching your custom design with precision and care.

⏱️ Timeline depends on complexity: 10-35 working days. We provide updates on progress.

6

Quality Check & Final Payment

Before shipping, we perform a thorough quality check. You'll receive photos of the finished blouse, and we collect the remaining 50% payment.

✅ Every stitch inspected. Your approval before shipment.

7

Delivery & After-Care Support

Your custom blouse is carefully packaged and shipped to you. We provide detailed care instructions and after-care support.

📦 Secure packaging with tracking. Lifetime care consultation available.

Important Notes

Limited Capacity: We accept a limited number of orders per week to maintain our quality standards.

Event-Date Assurance: If you have a specific event date, let us know early. We may be able to prioritize your order for an additional fee.

Design Confirmations: Timelines begin only after you approve the design and make the booking payment.

Custom Work: Each blouse is handcrafted to order. No stock items—everything is made specifically for you.
