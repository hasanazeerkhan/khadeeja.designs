Delivery Timelines & Event-Date Assurance

We understand that handcrafted Aari work is often tied to once-in-a-lifetime moments. Our timelines are structured to respect your event dates without compromising craftsmanship.

Service Timelines

Service Type Typical Timeline Notes
Simple Aari Blouse 10–14 working days Design confirmation required
Medium / Bridal Aari Blouse 18–25 working days Depends on stitch density
Heavy Bridal / Couture Work 25–35 working days Includes detailed hand embroidery
Saree Aari Work 15–25 working days Based on motif spread
Event-Date Priority Orders Case-based Priority slot + additional charges

How Our Timeline Works

  • • Timelines begin only after design confirmation and booking payment
  • • Changes requested after confirmation may affect delivery date
  • • Event-date priority is subject to capacity availability
  • • We intentionally limit weekly orders to maintain quality

Unlike informal tailoring setups, Khadeeja Designs operates on planned production schedules. We commit only to timelines we can honor. Your event date matters as much as our reputation.
