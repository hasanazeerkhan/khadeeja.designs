Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

Khadeeja Designs ("we", "our", "us") is a boutique Aari embroidery studio based in Trichy, Tamil Nadu. We are committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we handle it.

What data we collect

Information you give us directly:

  • Your name and mobile number when you submit an enquiry form
  • Your design brief and any message you include in the form
  • Any information you share when you contact us via WhatsApp

Data collected automatically through third-party tools:

  • Pages visited on our website and time spent on each page
  • Device and browser type
  • General location at city or region level — not your exact address

Why we collect it

  • To respond to your enquiry and provide a quote
  • To understand how visitors use our site so we can improve it
  • To show relevant advertisements to people who have visited our site through Meta Ads and Google Ads

Third-party tools we use

Meta Pixel: We use Meta Pixel to understand which pages visitors view before enquiring, and to show relevant ads on Facebook and Instagram. You can opt out at facebook.com/settings.

Google Analytics: We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site. You can opt out using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Formspree: When you submit our enquiry form, data is processed by Formspree and delivered to our email. See Formspree's privacy policy for details.

How we store your data

Your enquiry details are received by email and stored only as long as needed to process your order. We do not store your data in any database or third-party CRM. We do not share your name, phone number, or design brief with any external parties other than those listed above.

Your rights

You have the right to ask us to delete any personal information you have shared with us. Contact us at khadeejadesigns@gmail.com and we will remove your data within 7 working days.

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDP Act), you have the right to access, correct, and request erasure of your personal data.

Cookies

Our site uses cookies placed by Meta Pixel and Google Analytics. These help these services recognise returning visitors. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect some site functionality.
